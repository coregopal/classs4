Story Summary
This Hindi narrative celebrates Holi as a joyful festival of colors enjoyed by all Indians but warns against excessive playfulness that can harm others, like throwing color-filled balloons. It recounts a boy's accidental injury to his grandfather with a balloon on Holi evening, leading to remorse and a neighborhood shift to safer color-only celebrations, stressing sharing happiness without causing pain.​
