Story Summary
This Hindi story recounts a young boy's playful Holi mischief that accidentally injures his grandfather. On Holi evening, after colors run out, the boy and friends climb the rooftop to throw leftover water-filled balloons. One hits a man on the street, breaking his glasses and lodging glass in his eye, requiring surgery. The boy later discovers the victim is his own दादाजी (grandfather), leading to deep remorse. He vows never to throw balloons again, and the neighborhood now celebrates Holi only with colors, emphasizing joy-sharing over harm.​
