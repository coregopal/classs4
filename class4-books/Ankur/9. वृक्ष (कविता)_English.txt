मिट॒टी को कुछ ठीक बनाकर
The children prepared the soil with care,

बच्चों ने एक बीज लगाया,
and gently planted a tiny seed there.

उस पर बरखा रानी बरसीं
The rain queen showered her grace from above,

उसको पानी खूब पिलाया।
and gave it plenty of water and love.

बीत गए जब दिन दो-चार
After two or four days had gone by,

अंकुर निकला ऊपर आया,
a sprout emerged and reached toward the sky.

उसने जब यह दुनिया देखी
When it first looked around this earth,

चारों तरफ़ अजूबा पाया।
it found a world of wonder and mirth.

बच्चों ने जब देखा पौधा
When the children saw the little plant grow,

उन्हें समझ में आई बात,
a truth began to dawn, they’d know—

इसे अगर है हमें बढ़ाना
“If we wish to see it thrive and stand,”

देनी होगी इसको खाद।
“we must feed it with care and fertile sand.”

बच्चों की सेवा से आखिर
Through the children’s devoted care,

पौधे में आ गईं टहनियाँ
the plant grew branches fine and fair.

उन पर पत्ते-फूल भी निकले
Leaves and blossoms gently unfurled,

दिखने लगी रंगीन तितलियाँ।
and colorful butterflies kissed the world.

जैसे जैसे वह पेड़ बना
As the plant turned into a tree so wide,

फैली एक नई हरियाली
a wave of green spread far and wide.

घने वृक्ष के नीचे रुककर
Beneath its shade a traveller stayed,

राहगीर ने छाया पा ली।
and found cool rest in the home it made.

बच्चे भी जब बड़े हो गए
When the children too grew tall and wise,

पढ़-लिखकर जानी यह बात
they learned a truth that opened their eyes—

मानव और प्रकृति दोनों को
That man and nature, side by side,

जीना होगा हरदम साथ।
must live together, hearts allied.
